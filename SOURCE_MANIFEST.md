## 1. Provenance & Synthetic Data Declaration

All data processed and generated by this engine is **100% synthetically generated** using deterministic pseudorandom generators parameterized by banking domain distributions.

- **No Public/Proprietary PII:** No personally identifiable information (PII), confidential banking files, or proprietary scoring weights are used or redistributed.
- **Lawful and Ethical Provenance:** Synthetic applicant names and company entities are constructed from standard public distribution lists. National IDs are deterministically generated pseudonymized SHA-256 hashes.
- **Fair Lending Guardrail:** Protected demographic attributes (`gender`, `race`, `ethnicity`, `religion`, `marital_status`) are structurally excluded from underwriting decision logic, guaranteeing compliant credit evaluation.
